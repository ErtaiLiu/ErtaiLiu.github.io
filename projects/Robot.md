# PhytoPatholoBots

These specialized robots provide comprehensive plant health management through integrated detection and treatment systems designed for high-value crops.

## Project Overview

PhytoPatholoBots (PPBs) represent the next evolution in agricultural robotics, combining disease detection with targeted intervention capabilities. While traditional agricultural robots focus on either monitoring or broad treatment applications, PPBs integrate both functions into a unified, autonomous system.

## Key Capabilities

### 1. Multi-Pathogen Detection

PPBs can identify and distinguish between:

- Fungal infections (powdery mildew, rust, botrytis)
- Bacterial diseases (bacterial spot, crown gall, fire blight)
- Viral infections (mosaic viruses, leaf curl)
- Nutrient deficiencies (nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, micronutrients)
- Pest damage (insects, mites, nematodes)

### 2. Targeted Treatment Delivery

Each robot is equipped with:

- Precision spray system with droplet size control (20-150 microns)
- Multiple treatment reservoirs for different solutions
- UV-C light treatment array for non-chemical interventions
- Mechanical removal tools for infected plant parts
- Beneficial organism applicators for biological control

### 3. Decision Support System

The onboard AI makes real-time treatment decisions based on:

- Disease type and severity
- Plant growth stage
- Weather conditions
- Treatment history
- Economic thresholds
- Organic vs. conventional protocols

## Technical Specifications

### Physical Characteristics

- Dimensions: 75cm × 60cm × 55cm (adjustable height)
- Weight: 35kg (base unit without consumables)
- Ground clearance: 15-45cm (adjustable)
- Power system: 48V LiFePO4 battery (8-10 hour runtime)
- Charging: Automated docking station with solar option

### Sensing Systems

- 4K RGB cameras with 10x optical zoom
- Thermal imaging sensor (-20°C to 120°C range)
- Multispectral camera (5 bands)
- Environmental sensors (temperature, humidity, light, wind)
- 3D sensing for plant structure mapping

### Treatment Systems

- 4-channel precision spray system (2L capacity per channel)
- UV-C light array (adjustable intensity)
- Pneumatic precision pruning tool
- Electrostatic sprayer for beneficial organism application
- Hot air treatment module for specific fungal diseases

## Deployment Results

### Vineyard Trial (Napa Valley, CA)

- 93% reduction in powdery mildew incidence
- 76% reduction in chemical inputs
- 4.2% increase in quality rating of harvested grapes
- ROI achieved in 2.1 growing seasons

### Apple Orchard Implementation (Washington State)

- 82% reduction in fire blight spread
- 54% reduction in conventional fungicide use
- Estimated labor savings of 267 hours per season
- Quality improvements resulting in 7% price premium

### Greenhouse Tomato Production

- 95% early detection rate for gray mold
- 89% reduction in crop losses
- 79% reduction in manual scouting time
- Complete elimination of preventative broad-spectrum treatments

## Current Limitations

- High initial capital cost ($75,000-$95,000 per unit)
- Limited treatment options for certain viral diseases
- Requires specialized training for setup and maintenance
- Weather constraints for some field operations
